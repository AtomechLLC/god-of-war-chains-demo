God of War Chains

Reverse-engineering God of War 1 (PS2, 2005): the Kratos model, animation system, combo chains, and Blades of Chaos — decoded from the disc and rebuilt as a browser lab.

kratos-lab — model, animation & combo tester

Textured, skinned, animated Kratos in a meter-grid arena with a PS2 button pad and a combo sequence builder (live branch preview). Everything runs on decoded disc data: the swordtrail swoosh decal, chain-link/glow geometry from the real Segment Length & diameters, per-attack root motion (the hidden ANM comp-422 controller channel — combo3A 1.74 m … combo3F 5.26 m), the authored Concussion hit volumes (Plume's 2 m AoE + 4.5 m shockwave ring), Rage of the Gods with the real god-mode FX set, and the stage-5 blades with the decoded red L5 Rage trail. Toggle "Hitboxes" for the swept hit-volume display.

Designer tweak sheets (.twk)

Browsable in-page viewer: the decoded-from-disc sheets (Player, weapon levels, costumes, Concussion attack hit volumes, TweakTemplates — all values real) plus one reconstructed sheet per combat animation with provenance-tagged real vs inferred data.

About game assets: the viewer runs live here on a small curated subset of extracted data (~10 MB — the hero model, textures, animations, and weapon FX records) for research/preservation purposes. The full extracted set is not distributed; to regenerate everything from your own disc, see the repo docs.
